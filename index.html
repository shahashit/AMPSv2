The vehicle has changed

Electric, connected, software-defined. The service floor must follow.

EVs, hybrids, electric vans and trucks bring high voltage, cameras, radar, sensors and networked electronics into every bay. Mechanical skill now meets electrical, electronic and digital work. AMPS programme teams are trained for this mix, and document it digitally, so your service operation evolves with the vehicles it services.

1 Front camera and radarADAS calibration after windscreen, bumper or alignment work.
2 Surround cameras and sensorsParking, lane and blind-spot systems to test and document.
3 Roof sensors and connectivityOver-the-air updates, telematics and software coding.
4 High-voltage batteryIsolation, safety routines and battery diagnostics.
5 Electric drive and inverterThermal management and drive-unit service.
6 Charging systemCharge port, on-board charger and cable checks.
7 Networked electronicsDozens of control units talking over vehicle networks.

Where the industry is heading

The future of repair is being built on real work.

Carmakers and research teams already learn from skilled hands and real service data. Businesses that capture their work today are ready when these tools arrive, without having to bet on them now.

Germany July 2026
Tesla records skilled workers to train its Optimus robot Selected employees in Grünheide wear cameras that record how they grip tools and handle parts, to teach a humanoid robot the same tasks. Source: electrive, citing Handelsblatt ↗
Germany 2022–2024
Service floors collect real data to train diagnostic AI In the federally funded "Autowerkstatt 4.0" project, service businesses were invited to collect real measurement data to train AI for vehicle diagnostics. Source: Autoservicepraxis ↗

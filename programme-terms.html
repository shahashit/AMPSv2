Programme terms

Scoped per programme. Steered on milestones.

AMPS runs aftersales transformation programmes, sold only as a project or service with a defined scope, milestones, deliverables, duration and an AMPS team lead. You agree what the programme will deliver, not a number of people or hours.

Pricing

Scoped per programme

Set out in your written proposal. It depends on the bays, repair areas, sites, duration and roadmap steps agreed with you.

Deliverables

Results, not hours

Agreed work done, digital documentation of every job, a baseline report, monthly milestone reports and a quarterly review.

Data use

Agreed in writing

The capture scope and the use of the anonymised data are written into every client agreement before work starts.

Agreement

One written agreement

Scope, milestones, deliverables and data use in one agreement, with your AMPS programme lead as day-to-day contact.

Team lead

One named contact

An AMPS team lead, on site or remote, plans and runs the AMPS team and is your single point of contact.

Optional

AMPS Repair Library

Not included in the programme price and priced separately, on request. Only anonymised captures are used.

How we get to a signed programme.

Request a programme proposal. We hold a scoping call, define scope and milestones with you, send a written proposal including a data-use clause, conclude the agreement, and then prepare the team and kit and start the programme.

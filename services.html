Repair areas

Six repair areas. Every one ready for the next step.

Each area covers repair, maintenance and service work that the AMPS team takes on in agreed bays. Every job is documented digitally from day one, and each area has its own next steps: assistance on complex work, insight from your data and, with partners, automation. Service & maintenance

Scheduled maintenance and service, APK / MOT preparation, brakes and suspension. The same tasks repeat across brands, sites and dealer networks, which makes digital documentation pay off fastest here, and makes this the area where automation is expected to start.

Why the data is worth learning from.

Good documentation starts with work done well. The work is carried out by AMPS team members employed by AutoMPS LLP, working to one standard under an AMPS team lead, so results from different repair areas, sites and networks can be compared.

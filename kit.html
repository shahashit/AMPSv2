From the bay to the report

What happens to what is captured.

Four steps, always in this order. Nothing is processed before it has been anonymised, and your own staff never have to edit anything.

  1. 01 Capture Tablet, phone or glasses record the job in agreed bays, while the work is done.
  2. 02 Anonymise Faces, number plates, documents and screens are removed before anything else.
  3. 03 Structure Jobs are split into steps, labelled and timed by the AMPS back office.
  4. 04 Report You receive documentation per job and insight in the monthly milestone report.
How the kit is managed

Our devices, our responsibility.

Every device in the AMPS technology is owned, set up and managed by AMPS. Your service operation provides the bays and the work; we take care of the tools and what they capture.

Owned and set up by AMPS Devices are delivered configured, charged and tested.
Managed remotely Locked, updated and wiped remotely if a device is lost.
Agreed zones only Capture happens in the agreed bays, never in offices or reception.
Facing the work Glasses and cameras face the job, not the people.
Encrypted upload Captures are encrypted in transit and at rest.
Site survey first Bays, lighting, charging and excluded areas checked before day one.

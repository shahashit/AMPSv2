Careers · AMPS programme technician

Repair real vehicles.
Build the future of vehicle service.

As an AMPS programme technician you carry out real repair, maintenance and service work at service sites in the Netherlands, Belgium, France and Germany, and document every job with tablet, phone and smart glasses. You grow with the programme into digital tools, EV, ADAS, insight and automation. AutoMPS LLP employs you; your local AMPS team helps you travel, settle in and feel at home.

Apply now: 5 minutes → See open programme roles
The role

Programme technician in a aftersales transformation programme.

Each AMPS programme has a defined scope, milestones and an AMPS team lead. Your job has two parts: do good repair, maintenance and service work, and help the service floor modernise by documenting every job and working with new tools as they arrive.

Repair real vehicles Service, diagnostics, EV, tyres, body or heavy-vehicle work in the repair areas agreed for the programme.
Document every job Smart glasses record hand-eye movements while you work; the tablet logs repair times, tools, key moments and decision paths.
Trained in anonymisation You learn what may be recorded, and recordings are anonymised before processing: people, number plates and customer information are removed.
Build the future of repair Your work becomes insight that helps aftersales operations modernise, step by step towards automation.
Employee benefits

As your employer, AMPS takes care of the practical side.

Moving to another country for work is a big step. AMPS arranges contract, permits, housing, insurance and language lessons, so you can arrive prepared, settle in quickly and concentrate on the work.

Contract & salary An employment contract with AutoMPS LLP and your salary paid on time, every month.
Work & residence permit We prepare and file the paperwork, tax registration and municipal registration.
Housing arranged Decent, furnished accommodation near your service operation, ready when you arrive.
Insurance Occupational accident and leisure insurance, plus help with healthcare registration.
Travel & arrival Travel planned, pick-up on arrival and a first-week programme to find your way.
Language lessons Dutch, French or German lessons during your programme, free of charge.
Local AMPS contact A person in your programme country who speaks your language and checks in regularly.
Next programmes When a programme ends, we look for your next one, so your career keeps moving.

Training & preparation

Trained on the kit, briefed to settle in.

Before your first job in a programme, AMPS trains you on the AMPS technology and method: tablet documentation, smart glasses, and how to respect privacy and anonymise.

01AMPS technology trainingWorking with smart glasses and a tablet: what to record, how to log repair steps and times.
02Privacy & anonymisationWhat may and may not be recorded, and how recordings are anonymised before processing.
03Pre-departure briefingCountry, service floor culture, safety rules and what to expect in week one.
04Welcome & check-insIntroduction on site, then regular check-ins with your AMPS programme lead and local contact.

About AMPS

We bring aftersales to its next step.

AMPS stands for Automotive Maintenance & Physical-AI Services. We run aftersales transformation programmes in the Netherlands, Belgium, France and Germany: a dedicated AMPS team takes care of today’s work in agreed bays and moves the service floor forward step by step, from digital documentation to insight, assisted repair and, with partners, automation.

Request a programme proposal → The future of repair →
Our missionAMPS exists to help dealers, retailers and independents meet today’s challenges and tomorrow’s opportunities together: capacity now, a modern aftersales next.
Our nameAMPS: Automotive Maintenance & Physical-AI Services. Today’s work and tomorrow’s service site, in one name.
Our premiseEvery step builds on the last: documentation first, then insight and assistance, then automation when it makes sense. Physical AI is the long-term horizon, not the starting point.
Our promise“Every repair done well. Every repair recorded openly, and anonymised before anything else happens to it.”
What we do

A modernisation programme, run as a project.

Every AMPS programme is a project with a defined scope, milestones, deliverables, duration and an AMPS team lead. The agreed work gets done and every job is documented, so the service floor can take its next step.

Capture Real work, documented. Hand-eye coordinated movements, repair times, key repair moments and decision paths are recorded with tablets, smart glasses and video during real repair, maintenance and service work. The service floor gets that work done as a by-product.
Anonymise & structure Privacy first. Faces, number plates and customer information are removed before anything is processed, and data is never used to assess individual staff.
Goal A roadmap to automation. From tablet to insight, assistance and, with partners, automation: step by step, at the service floor’s pace.
Route to scale

Working with OEMs and dealer groups.

The same method works for one site, a dealer group or a whole network. That is why we work towards partnerships with manufacturers, importers, large dealer groups and technology partners, so programmes can grow from one site to many.

OEMs & importers One method across the network. The same method, documentation and reporting at every dealer, aligned with your catalogue repair times and maintenance schedules.
Dealer groups Insight across your sites. Agreed work done, repair-time insight per site and a roadmap towards automation.
One method Comparable results, every site. The same method, documentation and reporting wherever the programme runs, so one site can be compared with the next.
Meet AutoMPS EU

The local team that scopes and runs your programme.

AutoMPS EU is the European representation of AutoMPS LLP. With field coordinators in the Netherlands, Belgium, France and Germany, we are the people you meet, call and plan with before, during and after every programme.

01 Programme scoping We turn your goals into a clear programme: repair areas, brands, sites, timing, milestones and deliverables.
02 Programme leadership Every programme has a named AMPS programme lead for proposals, reporting, reviews and escalation.
03 On-site coordination Field coordinators visit the service floor, attend start-up days and run regular check-ins with you and the team.
04 Team and kit set-up Team lead appointed, tablets and smart glasses issued, and your floor team briefed before the first day.
05 Milestone reporting Monthly milestone reports and quarterly reviews with your service operation or HQ.
06 Compliance liaison Coordination with AutoMPS LLP on data terms, permits and local employment rules for our own employees.

The principles we work by.

01Transparent upfrontClients know what is documented, where and why before the first job. Nothing is filmed covertly.
02Anonymised firstPeople, number plates and customer information are removed before any footage or log is processed.
03Informed and agreedCapture scope and data use are written into every client agreement, and our technicians know exactly what they capture.
04Responsible automationTechnology should support skilled people. Automation comes step by step, starting with routine tasks, with safety and repair quality first.
05Repair quality firstCapture runs alongside the job. The vehicle in the bay is repaired properly, whatever is being recorded.
06AccountableOne programme agreement, one programme lead, clear deliverables and regular reporting.
07Fully compliantEvery programme respects local employment and safety rules, and its data terms are agreed in writing.
08Care for peopleDecent housing, insurance and language courses for every AMPS technician.

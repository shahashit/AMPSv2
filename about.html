Why AMPS exists

Three shifts. One programme answers all three.

Vehicles changed faster than service floors. AMPS closes that gap with capacity, data and a clear path forward, in one accountable programme.

The vehicle Electric, connected, software-defined. High voltage, cameras, radar and networked electronics now reach every bay.
The service floor More work than hours. Demand keeps growing while capacity, skills and time stay tight.
The tools Data and AI arrive. Know-how that is not captured today is lost tomorrow.
The AMPS answer Capacity today. Data every day. A modern aftersales next. One programme, one programme lead, clear milestones and monthly reporting.
How AMPS is built

Four building blocks around your service floor.

Every programme combines the same four blocks. That is what makes results comparable from one site to the next, and scalable from one site to a network.

Programme teamA dedicated, AMPS-led team with a named programme lead, trained on mechanical, electrical and EV work.
AMPS method and SOPsOne way of working, documenting and reporting, on every job and at every site.
TechnologyTablet first, then smart glasses, connected tools, AI documentation and partner automation.
Insight and reportingMonthly milestone reports, quarterly reviews and insight from your own anonymised data.

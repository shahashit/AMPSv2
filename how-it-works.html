The programme

Four phases. Clear milestones. Visible progress.

A dedicated AMPS programme team takes on agreed work in your bays, turns every job into data and moves your aftersales forward step by step. You steer on milestones; we take care of the team, the technology and the reporting.

Configure your programme → Request a programme proposal →
How it runs

From first call to a transforming service floor.

Phase 1 · Week 0 Discover A scoping call and site survey: your sites, bays, vehicle mix, service areas and ambition.
✓Scope and milestones agreed✓Privacy and works council set-up✓Written programme proposal
Phase 2 · Month 1 Launch The programme team, programme lead and technology are in place. Your floor team is briefed.
✓Programme lead appointed✓Tablets and smart glasses ready✓Floor team and works council briefed
Phase 3 · Ongoing Run and capture Agreed work is done in agreed bays, and every job is captured as photo, video, time and data.
✓Agreed work done✓Every job documented digitally✓Monthly milestone report
Phase 4 · Step by step Transform Data becomes insight, assisted repair and, with partners, automation. Each step is proposed at the quarterly review.
✓Baseline report in month 3✓Smart glasses and AI insight✓Automation pilots with partners
From tablet to robotics

How your aftersales evolves, milestone by milestone.

Every programme starts at milestone 1 and shows results within months. Each next milestone starts when the previous one delivers, agreed and reviewed with you. We don’t commit to a pace; we commit to the journey.

Milestone 1 · Start here Digitalise The programme team takes on agreed work in your bays and documents every job on tablet from day one. You get capacity, proof and a baseline of how work really flows.

Simple roles

You set the direction. We run the programme.

Your part →Agree scope, milestones and ambition→Provide the agreed bays→Set priorities through your work orders→Brief your floor team and works council
The AMPS part ✓A dedicated programme team, composed and led by AMPS✓An AMPS programme lead as your single contact✓The AMPS method and technology on every job✓Capture, anonymisation, insight and reporting✓Training on new technology and EV know-how✓Partners for each next step on the roadmap
Programme formats

Start with one site. Scale to a network.

Each extension is its own decision, taken when the results are there.

Starter One site A few bays, milestone 1 live, milestone 2 ready. Site-level reporting.
Growth Group or chain One method across sites and brands, group reporting, milestones 1 to 3.
Network National or OEM network One standard across many sites, dashboards, partner pilots.

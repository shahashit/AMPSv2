Who we work with

Built for authorised dealers, retailers and leading independents.

From a single independent site to a dealer group with many sites or an OEM network. Each programme is scoped to your brands, bays and repair areas, takes care of today’s work and moves your service operation forward, step by step.

01 Dealer groups → 02 Leading independent chains → 03 Body & damage repair → 04 Tyres & fast-fit → 05 Truck, bus & heavy vehicles → 06 OEMs & importers →
01 · Dealer groups

Your sites, ready for what comes next.

A programme can be scoped for one site or for many sites and brands of your group. The AMPS team takes on agreed work in agreed bays, and every site works with the same method, documentation and reporting.

Where your brands are part of an OEM programme, we align the scope and method with it. Group-level reporting lets you compare sites against each other and against OEM catalogue times.

Request a programme proposal → How the programme works
What your group gets
✓Agreed work done in the agreed bays, from the first day
✓Digital documentation of every job: photos, video, digital vehicle check
✓Repair-time insight per site, against OEM catalogue times
✓One method and one report across all sites
✓A roadmap towards smart-glass assistance and automation
02 · Leading independent chains

Multi-brand work, consistent across branches.

Independents service many brands with lean teams. An AMPS programme takes on agreed work and documents it the same way at every branch, so customers get a consistent, modern experience wherever they go.

Start with one branch and a few bays. Extend when the results are there.

Request a programme proposal → Technology
What the programme delivers
✓Agreed work done, to manufacturer method
✓Photo and video proof per job for your customers
✓Digital vehicle check on every visit
✓Monthly milestone report and quarterly review
✓The optional AMPS Repair Library, priced separately
03 · Body & damage repair

Documentation insurers ask for, from day one.

Insurers already expect photos and evidence for every claim. In an AMPS programme, damage, repair steps and quality checkpoints are documented in structured photo and video sets, as part of the work.

That makes claims faster to approve, repairs easier to verify and the fine-motor craft of your best people visible and teachable.

Request a programme proposal → Repair areas
What you get
✓Damage photo sets before and after every repair
✓Repair steps documented for insurers and customers
✓Quality checkpoints recorded per job
✓Insight into time per repair step
✓Smart repair and paint work captured as training material
04 · Tyres & fast-fit

High-volume work, first in line for automation.

Tyre changes, balancing, TPMS service and quick-service jobs are short, frequent and repeatable. Digital checks pay off immediately, and this is the kind of work where partner automation, such as drive-over scanners and tyre robotics, tends to arrive first.

Request a programme proposal → Future of repair
What we document
✓Digital tread-depth and pressure records
✓Cycle time per wheel and per car
✓Torque and pressure checks
✓Photo proof for the customer
✓Your data shows where an automation pilot pays off
05 · Truck, bus & heavy vehicles

Digital inspections for fleet uptime.

Maintenance, service and repair of trucks, buses and trailers: air brakes, hydraulics and commercial-vehicle electronics. Digital inspection reports and documented routines support uptime for fleet customers and safer work in the bay.

Request a programme proposal → Repair areas
What we document
✓Digital inspection reports for fleet customers
✓Brake, pressure and leak tests recorded
✓Time per inspection and repair step
✓Heavy-part handling routines
✓Air and hydraulic fault paths
06 · OEMs & importers

One programme across your dealer network.

AMPS works with the manufacturer or importer to define one programme: brands, repair areas, documentation standard, milestones and deliverables. The OEM then offers that programme into its dealer network, so every participating dealer works with the same method.

Each dealer agrees the scope and data terms for its own site in writing. Anonymised, network-wide insight supports aftersales quality and prepares the network for automation, together with technology partners.

Request a programme proposal → Partner ecosystem
What the network gets
✓One method and documentation standard across dealers
✓Network-wide insight, aligned with your catalogue repair times
✓Capacity support for participating dealers
✓A shared roadmap towards automation with partners
✓Training material through the AMPS Repair Library
